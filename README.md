# Dead Pines

*An open-world survival horror game about being alone in the woods.*

You wake up somewhere random with nothing. No map. No compass. No HUD, no minimap, no
tutorial, no quest marker. You learn the land because nothing else survives with you —
which ridge leads where, how far the sound of a river carries, which treeline is hiding a
road. How close to civilization you're willing to go is the only real decision you ever make.

The feeling we're chasing is **isolation**. Wind in the trees. Birds, and then quiet. Your own
footsteps on gravel. And the particular dread of maybe seeing another person.

## Inspirations

| Game | What we take from it |
| --- | --- |
| **Infestation: Survivor Stories** | The core loop — and above all its ambience. Environmental sound design as the primary storytelling tool, in a world that is mostly not happening to you |
| **PUBG** | The tension of a world where every encounter is a decision. Nothing forces a fight; the map creates the reasons |
| **Arc Raiders** | Extraction-shooter pacing, and its pre-defined callout system as a way to communicate without a chat box |
| **The Long Dark** | Location structure — each place a self-contained scene with its own lighting and its own reasons to be there |
| **Firewatch** | Proof that a small team's worth of trees, lit and fogged with discipline, reads as a forest. The look comes from light and palette, not from asset count |

The through-line is that the *world* is the antagonist, and it never announces itself.

## Design pillars

1. **Sound carries the game.** Everything dangerous is audible before it's visible.
2. **Isolation over population.** Encounters are rare, and always chosen.
3. **Bots are the game, humans are a bonus.** Fully playable and populated offline.
4. **Knowledge is the only progression.** Nothing carries between lives but what you learned.
5. **Calm is the contrast** that makes danger mean anything.

## What it is

Single-player, PC, offline. A local persistent world with its own population, which means no
servers, no matchmaking, and no waiting for anyone else to be online. Loot respawns on timers,
the dead stay where they fell, and night is a place you cross rather than a thing that happens
to you.

Built by one person in the hours around a day job, with free and CC0 assets only. With no art
budget, the look has to come from lighting, fog and silhouette — which, conveniently, is also
the honest way to make a forest frightening.

## Status

**Pre-production.** The engine project exists — Unity 6.3 LTS, URP, Windows — and it is
currently empty, which is the correct state for it.

**No screenshots yet.** The forest doesn't exist. The next milestone is a walkable one: a
forest at dusk you can walk through for five minutes without getting bored, with three
performance numbers written down and a budget to hold every later milestone against.

Follow along, or don't — it'll be here.
